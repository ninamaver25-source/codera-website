# The film — scene plan

Layout: desktop — the words left of the laptop, level with the lower half of its screen; phones —
the words above the laptop, never touching it, the office filling the whole screen.

One room from the first frame to the last — a dark, premium architectural office: warm charcoal
walls, dark walnut, cognac leather, warm amber light, a little daylight from the window; no blue
light, no neon. Type: Montserrat light + bold for headlines, Geist for text, ivory (#F2EBE1) on
warm charcoal (#15110E), amber (#D6A066) as the only accent. Slow, controlled, realistic: the
camera moves through the same room; devices appear, screens change; nothing flies in. The
headlines sit on the empty wall, never over the chair or the desk. No blocks and no section edges:
the end of the film is the same colour as the page after it.
